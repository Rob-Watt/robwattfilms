about

Rob Watt is a filmmaker whose primary focus is narrative features.

He attended Loyola Marymount University, where he was encouraged to enter film by William Fulco, S.J., Ph.D. Since that day, Rob has worked with talented mentors and film legends.

His inspirations include Terrence Malick, Mel Gibson, Akira Kurosawa, and Martin Scorsese.

In 2008, Rob co-founded SOLA GRATIA along with Grey Damon, to champion high concept, narrative feature films.

Inspired by Truffaut's interviews of Hitchcock, Rob founded the Getting Made Podcast, where he seeks to learn from fellow filmmakers from their experiences, on and off set.
